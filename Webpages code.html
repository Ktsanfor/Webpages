Welcome to my about me page!

One of my favorite parts of campus is the walk from Talley Student Union to the Brickyard. I usually like to take the path that goes through Talley first, weaving past the shops and dining areas inside. From there, I head toward the Free Expression Tunnel, which is always covered in colorful, ever-changing artwork and messages from students. Walking through the tunnel feels like stepping into a small gallery before coming back out into the open air. By the time I reach the Brickyard, I've had a nice mix of scenery, art, and campus life all in one short walk.
